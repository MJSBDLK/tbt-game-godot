# Traveller

## In a line
> A traveling peasant who picked up his pickaxe to fight. No formal training. He's a miner by trade, and a bit of a nomad. Has basic navigation skills.

## How do they fight?
> uses a pickaxe. Throws a hatchet as his ranged move. Has a lantern and a napsack for support.

## How do they help or hinder without hitting?
> Lantern and napsack come into play here, napsack will be central to the cast animation.

## Stats
> HP: med/low
> Strength: average
> Special: med/low
> Skill: low
> Agility: med/low
> Athleticism: slightly above average
> Defense: slightly below average
> Resistance: medium
> Anything else they're good or bad at: navigation, and they can illuminate through fog with their lantern. They're harder to ambush, but they're not hyper-vigilant or anything like that. They're worldly for a peasant and have been robbed/mugged/pickpocketed before, so they're wary and harder to trick. Also relatively courageous.

## How do they react when they...
> drop a foe: they aren't used to killing and they handle it almost like they're in shock: they'll deal with the emotional weight of this later - for now, there's still danger, so we press on
> lose an ally: they're seasoned by the road, and rarely have companionship, so they aren't used to losing companions either. they're relatively composed/courageous, but this hits them considerably harder than a trained soldier.

## What would they never do?
> settle down in once place, at least until they're too old for the road.
> overpay for something that wasn't essential, or pass up a good deal - they have a good sense for barter and aren't easily manipulated
> swindle others: Traveler is an honest type.

## How do they move?
> They're in relatively good shape, but not agile/graceful: he's thin and wiry, with a natural, albeit untrained grace to him. Too young to have many serious injuries or achy joints yet, and leads a very active lifestyle.

## Like
> I'm reminded a bit of Domhnall of Zena from Dark Souls - Traveler isn't quite as jovial, he's a very non-grumpy, roll-with-the-punches type in terms of his personality
