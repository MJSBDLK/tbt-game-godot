# Squash

## In a line
> Hired muscle. He's twisted an arm or two in his life.

## How do they fight?
> He's actually not that strong - stronger than most, to be sure, but more than strength, he's HEAVY and difficult to move. Exremely high constitution. Kind of a grappler type. Think a bouncer, who could literally bounce you out of the club with his gut - he's a wall. He's "the" wall.

## How do they help or hinder without hitting?
> slaps his belly and laughs sinisterly.

## Stats
> HP very high:
> Strength: above average
> Special: low
> Skill: low
> Agility: extremely low
> Athleticism: low (he's huge, and fat, but not slovenly - he's very physical in his work)
> Defense (cuts physical damage taken): high
> Resistance (cuts special damage taken): above average
> Anything else they're good or bad at: He can take a clobbering and keep going - that's his forté.

## How do they react when they...
> land a big hit: gloat
> lose an ally: unphased - he doesn't have many real friends anyway - they might've stabbed him in the back later
> get surrounded: confident - it'll take a lot to bring him down

## What would they never do?
> lift a finger without pay
> reveal a secret they have an incentive to keep, except under the most extreme duress
> let someone through he was paid to keep out

## How do they move?
> Heavy, stiff, proud, strong.

## How do they take a hit, and how do they go down?
> Takes it off the belly, laughs
> Big things fall harder

## Other characteristics
> Nicer clothes, gold rings, usually stationed in a slum, to keep the rabble in line. Is paid well for his valuable services, and would accept no less. Usually doesn't need to use force: a staredown is enough.
