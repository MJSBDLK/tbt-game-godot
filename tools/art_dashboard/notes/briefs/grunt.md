# Grunt

## In a line
> Cannon fodder-type enemy. Not without strengths, but they're not particularly threatening.

## How do they fight?
> With a basic polearm. They are lightly armored, relatively slow and unskilled.

## How do they help or hinder without hitting?
> they lend a helping hand, physically - zero magical prowess, unskilled laborers

## Stats
> HP: average
> Strength: average
> Special: low
> Skill: low
> Agility: low
> Athleticism: med-low
> Defense: slightly above average
> Resistance: slightly below average
> Anything else they're good or bad at: relatively courageous, but nothing special. They threaten units with weak physical attack and defense.

## How do they react when they...
> big hit, drop a foe: they're no nonsense about it. They might remark, but they're mostly business. they come from fairly tough stock, so little phases them, but they also lack much formal training.
> miss/get hurt/get surrounded/lose an ally: also no nonsense, but they might overreact in frustration/fear. They aren't panickers, but again, their lack of training and investment hurts them here: they aren't careerists.

## What would they never do?
> Read, be invited to a fancy dinner, or engage in an intellectual conversation. It's not that they wouldn't be interested in such things, but the hand they were dealt in life put them in a caste that was difficult to escape, and they never had opporunities to develop their capacities in such things.

## How do they move?
> stiff, un-hasty, reasonably decisive and no-nonsense.
