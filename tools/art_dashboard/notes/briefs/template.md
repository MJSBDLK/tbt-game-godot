## In a line
> Who are they?

## How do they fight?
> What do they hit with, from how close, and what's their job: hit hard, take
> hits, set others up, wreck the enemy's plans, or patch people up?

## How do they help or hinder without hitting?
> What do they use: a tool, an instrument, their voice, a trick, magic?
> Who gets it: themselves, an ally, a foe, everyone nearby?
> What's the one pose that sells it?

## Traits
> Each strength, then one or two flaws (ideally two), one line each:
> `strength: flaw, flaw`

## How do they react when they...
> Pick two or three: land a big hit / miss / get hurt / drop a foe / lose an
> ally / get surrounded / stand next to someone

## How do they move?
