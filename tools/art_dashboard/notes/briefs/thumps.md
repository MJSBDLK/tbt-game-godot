## In a line
> Thumps is a siamese twin with gigantism. The village took him in, and though he was viewed and treated as something of a freak, they were kinder to him than anyone else would have been. They put him to work doing the heavy lifting of the village, and he's respected for his strength and utility, but few actually want him around, deep down, but for his utility in construction, protection, etc. Thumps is grateful, but prone to emotional, destructive outbursts. His two halves also have different personalities. The right head likes to smash things, and the left half is more compassionate and would rather build. They are both unintelligent.

## How do they fight?
> Thumps fights with a hammer made from an anvil. He's more agile and athletic then he appears, making him a formidable threat. He's clumsy though, due to his two heads directing the body in imperfect coordination. He's resilient, but not incredibly so.

## How do they help or hinder without hitting?
> for the animation: he picks up a glowing rock (glow color dependent on the effect) with his giant hand, between his index finger and thumb, and gently touches it to the target.

## Stats
> HP (how much they can take): above average
> Strength (physical damage): high
> Special (special damage): low
> Skill (accuracy): extremely low
> Agility (dodging): below average
> Athleticism (out-speed a foe to hit twice): above average
> Defense (cuts physical damage taken): slightly above average
> Resistance (cuts special damage taken): average
> Anything else they're good or bad at: Has access to lots of multi-hit moves (which need to be lower-powered for balance, but each can have its own chance to crit/debuff)

## How do they react when they...
> land a big hit: the right head feels satisfaction, the left head does not like the violence but understands the necessity
> miss: the right head gets angry, the left head gets frustrated
> get hurt: right head shrugs it off, the left might panic
> get surrounded: the right head gets angry, the left gets scared
> lose an ally: the right is unfazed, the left is demoralized
> stand next to: right head is resolute, a man of action, left head loves company but is a bit of a doormat

## What would they never do?
> Read. The right head is borderline illiterate; the left head is more curious but lacks the cognitive ability to truly be knowledge seeking.

## How do they move?
> He's a heavyweight that's surprisingly difficult to get away from. Much more mobile than he looks. Moves kind of like a gorilla due to his longer arms, may transition between walking on two legs and using his hands to get around on rough terrain, as he's clumsy and prone to tripping. Also may get confused if both heads decide to go different directions. On the flip side, can be looking two directions at once, so he's really hard to get the drop on.
