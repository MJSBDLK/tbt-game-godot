Diagonal reach / line-of-sight explorer

A = attacker (fixed, center). Click any cell to toggle a wall (impassable). Hover a cell to make it the target T and see whether the attack reaches under each rule. Straight-line targets behave identically under all three — this only diverges on diagonals. The dashed circles show Extendo's actual case: the +1 ring (every tile at distance 2), green where reachable under the selected rule.

Target: hover a cell

Reach around (forgiving) —

Open if any minimal staircase of open tiles connects A to T. Only a fully-walled corner stops a diagonal. Generalizes to "is there an unobstructed L-path."

Any wall blocks (strict) —

Open only if every tile in the bounding box between A and T is open. Any wall in the gap stops it. Walls feel solid; positioning matters most.

True sightline (ray) —

A geometric ray from center to center. Only a wall whose interior the line actually crosses blocks it — corner-only touches slip through. Most permissive.

Tip: load "1 walled corner" and hover the diagonal tile (top-right of A). That single cell is the whole decision: forgiving & ray reach it, strict does not. Then try the "wall line" preset at longer range to see how ray diverges from the path-based rules.

Each rule maps directly to one GDScript implementation in GridGeometry: forgiving = monotone open-path search, strict = bounding-box all-clear, ray = segment vs open-square test. Pick the feel; I'll port that one and keep the others a drop-in swap.
