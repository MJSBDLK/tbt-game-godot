How to paint the twinkle layer

One pixel per star on a transparent layer. The pixel's color is instructions, not a color: every star renders in the star color above.

PaintResult
#FF0000X-shaped twinkle, tails reach 3 px
#880000X, tails reach 2 px. Darker = shorter reach; #440000 reaches 1 px
#00FF00+-shaped twinkle, same brightness rule
#FFFF00Alternates: X on one flash, + on the next
#FF00AAAny star plus blue = flashes more often. Blue 0 is the slow end, 255 the fast end (the cadence sliders)
#FFFFFFAlternating at the fastest cadence. White is a valid star
#0000FFBlue alone: no tails, the core just blinks
  • Each star's footprint is 7×7 at full reach. Stars closer than that overlap their tails, which is allowed but busy.
  • A tail's newest pixel is always the dimmest (the "tail tip pixel" lever). Pixels behind it brighten one step per pixel as the tail grows, and dim the same way as it retracts.
  • A dimmer painted star also rests dimmer (the "dimmest star at rest" lever sets the floor). Set that lever to 1 if every core should sit at full brightness.
  • Near-black or transparent pixels are empty sky. The dark background is its own layer underneath this one.
