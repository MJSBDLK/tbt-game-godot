Open questions

recommendation

Placement: full width on controller/kb, corners under touch

Full-width bottom row is the genre convention and on Deck's 640×400 the 14 px row lives in the spare 40. Under touch RQD prefers the corner clusters — with a corner inset: rounded screen corners clip flush content and the outermost few mm are touch-dead on many phones. Placement can follow the sampled input model, same as the glyphs do.

decide

Two controller actions are unbound

end_turn (E) and toggle_threat_zones (V) have keyboard bindings only. The bar can't hint what doesn't exist. Proposal shown dashed: X = End turn, L1 = Threat zones. ☰/Start → Menu is also proposed (today B opens the menu from DEFAULT via ui_cancel).

decide

Touch button height

14 px matches the bar but is ~2.5 mm on a 6.1" phone at 3×. A 7 mm target is ~40 canvas px. Toggle the tap footprint to see the gap. This is the physical-size question — the answer sets every touch control's size, not just these.

decide

Step text voice

Mocked in the Info voice (banana gold + YO 4 glow), uppercase, leftmost. Alternatives: primary azure (quieter, merges with verbs) or drop it under controller/kb and keep it only for touch, where there is nothing else to say.

decide

Enemy phase

Not an InputState — the turn owner flips. Bar shows the step text only ("Enemy phase") and no items. Alternative: hide the bar entirely.

note

What's deliberately not in the bar

Camera pan/zoom, tooltip peek (View / R3), and the unit-cycle L1/R1 idea. Five items is the ceiling per state; the bar teaches, it doesn't enumerate. Peek could ride UNIT_SELECTED if RQD wants it surfaced.

Decisions

  • Round 1 (2026-08-16): mocked. Awaiting RQD's picks on placement, touch height, step voice, and the two proposed controller bindings.
  • Round 2 (2026-08-16): RQD likes bottom corners on mobile, but not flush to the corner — their phone drops touches at the far corners and the rounded screen clips there. Added the corner-inset control (default 12 px) and the rounded bezel to the phone safe-area view. Leaning: placement follows input model (full-width under controller/kb, inset corners under touch).
