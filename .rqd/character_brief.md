# <Character>

**In a line:** who are they?

**How do they fight?** What do they hit with, from how close, and what's their
job: hit hard, take hits, set others up, wreck the enemy's plans, or patch
people up?

**How do they help or hinder without hitting?**
- What do they use: a tool, an instrument, their voice, a trick, magic?
- Who gets it: themselves, an ally, a foe, everyone nearby?
- What's the one pose that sells it?

**Traits:** each strength, then one or two flaws (ideally two).
- <strength>: <flaw>, <flaw>
- <strength>: <flaw>, <flaw>

**How do they react when they...** (pick two or three)
land a big hit / miss / get hurt / drop a foe / lose an ally / get surrounded /
stand next to someone

**What would they never do?**

**How do they move?** Heavy, quick, sloppy, stiff, proud? How do they take a
hit, and how do they go down?

**Like:** (optional) a character, animal or person that captures them.
